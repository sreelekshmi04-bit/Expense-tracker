How Smart Alerts Work

Unlike static spreadsheets, the app quietly watches category spending in real time.

You set a limit:
e.g. Food = ₹3,000 this month.
2.
You log expenses:

3.
App does the math:
Expenses − Budget = ₹400 overspend.
4.
Alert triggers immediately:
A warning is raised at 80% and overspend alert triggers at >100%.
